# 🦖 Kaiju Simulator 2

> **2nd Place Winner 🥈** at the IDMC Nancy Game Jam 2026 (organized with the EKOS association). 
> Developed from scratch in just **48 hours**!


## 🎮 About the Game
**Kaiju Simulator 2** is an action-packed platformer where you step into the shoes of a colossal Kaiju. Instead of saving the city, your sole objective is absolute destruction: smash skyscrapers, crush obstacles, and wreak havoc across the urban landscape while managing your rampage!

## 🏆 Context & Achievement
* **Event:** IDMC Game Jam 2026
* **Organizers:** IDMC (Institut des Sciences du Digital, Management et Cognition) & EKOS Association
* **Duration:** 48 hours (2 days)
* **Result:** **2nd Place** out of all participating teams! 🥈

## ✨ Key Features
* **Destructible Environment:** Smash buildings, smash infrastructure, and watch the city crumble beneath your claws.
* **Kaiju Abilities:** Unleash devastating attacks, tail swipes, and special powers to maximize chaos.
* **Arcade Platforming:** Fast-paced movement mechanics designed for smooth destruction flows.
* **Intense Atmosphere:** Dynamic visual and audio feedback emphasizing your sheer size and power.
